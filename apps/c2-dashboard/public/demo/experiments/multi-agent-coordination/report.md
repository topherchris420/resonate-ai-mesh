# Separation as the only coordination mechanism

Experiment `multi-agent-coordination`. Generated by `mesh` from `summary.json` and `runs.jsonl`. Every number is measured from recorded simulated runs; no text below was written by a model.

## Research question

In a crowded crossing, how does the minimum-separation rule trade conflicts for progress, and how do coherence and convergence respond?

## Hypothesis

A larger minimum separation keeps agents farther apart but rejects more proposals and lowers task success within the time limit.

Prediction: `task_success` will decrease from `separation_2` to `separation_4`.

- Scenario: `crowded-crossing`

## Limitations

- All inputs are simulated. Results describe this simulator and kernel configuration, not people or physical systems.
- Repetitions differ only by seed; they are not independent samples of real-world conditions.
- Mock judges are deterministic rules defined in this repository, not language models.
- Agents do not negotiate; conflicts are resolved by processing order and validation alone.
