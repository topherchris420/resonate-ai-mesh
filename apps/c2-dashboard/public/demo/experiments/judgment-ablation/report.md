# Judgment ablation on the perturbed mesh

Experiment `judgment-ablation`. Generated by `mesh` from `summary.json` and `runs.jsonl`. Every number is measured from recorded simulated runs; no text below was written by a model.

## Research question

Does a bounded judgment stage after deterministic validation change which proposals are committed, and at what cost in throughput and task progress?

## Hypothesis

With the evidence-heuristic judge, proposals made on degraded evidence (observations older than half the staleness limit) are withheld instead of committed. Replaying the recorded judgments reproduces the judged condition exactly. No judge can cause an unsafe commit, because judgment is only consulted after validation passes.

Prediction: `degraded_evidence_commits` will decrease from `deterministic_only` to `mock_judgment`.

## Limitations

- All inputs are simulated. Results describe this simulator and kernel configuration, not people or physical systems.
- Repetitions differ only by seed; they are not independent samples of real-world conditions.
- Mock judges are deterministic rules defined in this repository, not language models.
- The mock judges are deterministic rules in this repository; they show what a judgment stage does to the pipeline, not how a language model would judge.
- One scenario. Effects depend on how much stale evidence the scenario produces.
